## Chapter 1: What's All the Fuss About? Demystifying AI — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | What is Artificial Intelligence? | A set of technologies that enable computers to learn from experience and make decisions, simulating human-like intelligence in machines. |
| 2 | What is the relationship between AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning? | They are nested concepts: AI is the broadest category, Machine Learning is a subset of AI, and Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning. |
| 3 | What is Machine Learning? | A method where computers learn patterns from data and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed for each task. |
| 4 | What is Deep Learning? | A specialized subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with many layers to process complex patterns in large amounts of data. |
| 5 | When did the term "Artificial Intelligence" first emerge? | In 1956 at the Dartmouth Conference, where researchers formally established AI as a field of study. |
| 6 | What is the most common method AI systems use to improve? | Machine Learning — systems improve through exposure to data rather than manual programming. |
| 7 | Myth or Reality: AI thinks and reasons like humans do. | Myth. AI processes data and recognizes patterns but doesn't truly "think" or have consciousness like humans. |
| 8 | Why does AI matter in 2026? | AI is transforming industries, automating tasks, enabling personalized experiences, and creating new opportunities across healthcare, business, and daily life. |
| 9 | What are the "AI winters"? | Periods in AI history (1970s and late 1980s) when funding and interest declined due to unmet expectations and technical limitations. |
| 10 | Myth or Reality: AI will replace all human jobs. | Myth. AI typically augments human work, automates specific tasks, and creates new job categories rather than replacing entire professions. |
| 11 | What three things does an AI system need to learn effectively? | Data to learn from, algorithms to process patterns, and computing power to run calculations. |
| 12 | What breakthrough enabled the current AI boom starting around 2012? | Advances in Deep Learning, combined with big data availability and powerful GPUs for processing. |
| 13 | Myth or Reality: AI is just science fiction and magic. | Myth. AI is practical technology based on mathematics, statistics, and computer science principles. |
| 14 | How does Machine Learning differ from traditional programming? | Traditional programming uses explicit rules written by programmers; ML systems discover rules by analyzing patterns in data. |
| 15 | What is a neural network? | A computing system inspired by the human brain, consisting of interconnected nodes that process information in layers. |

### Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Artificial Intelligence (AI) | Technologies enabling computers to simulate human intelligence, learn from experience, and make decisions |
| Machine Learning (ML) | AI subset where systems learn and improve from data without explicit programming |
| Deep Learning | ML subset using multi-layered neural networks to process complex patterns |
| Neural Network | Computing architecture inspired by the brain, using connected nodes to process information |
| Algorithm | A set of rules or instructions that a computer follows to solve problems or make decisions |
| Training Data | The information used to teach a machine learning system to recognize patterns |
| AI Winter | Historical periods of reduced funding and interest in AI research due to unmet expectations |

### Memory Tricks
- **Nesting Dolls**: Picture Russian nesting dolls — AI is the biggest doll, ML fits inside it, and Deep Learning is the smallest doll inside ML.
- **"AI Learns, ML Earns"**: AI is the broad concept, but Machine Learning is what "earns" results by processing real data.
- **1956 = AI's Birthday**: Think "AI turned 70 in 2026" — the Dartmouth Conference was AI's official birth year.
